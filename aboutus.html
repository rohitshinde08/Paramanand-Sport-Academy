Who we are

About Us

Welcome to Parmanand Sports Academy, a premier sports academy dedicated to fostering athletic excellence and nurturing the next generation of champions. We are passionate about sports and committed to providing exceptional training programs that empower athletes to reach their full potential.

At Parmanand Sports Academy, we believe that sports have the power to transform lives. Whether you're a beginner looking to develop fundamental skills or an aspiring professional seeking advanced training, our academy offers a comprehensive range of programs suitable for athletes of all ages and skill levels.

Join us at Parmanand Sports Academy and embark on an incredible journey of athletic growth and achievement. Whether your passion lies in football, basketball, badminton, Gymnastics, or any other sport, we are here to support your dreams and help you soar to new heights.

Vision

Parmanand Sports Academy is already one of the top sports academy in Nashik. Equipped with state of the art equipment and international facilities in the most congenial atmosphere, we foresee ourselves as the frontrunners in the Sports arena. We envision to meet the increasing demands of the changing sports scenario and to earn a high reputation and utmost popularity for being one of the pioneer sports academies in India.

Mission

Our mission is to provide a wide range of sports and recreational activities to all sports lovers irrespective of their age. We aim to develop Sporty culture and holistic health for all. So Parmanand Sports Academy is committed to providing best in sports infrastructure and expert coaches to judge and carve distinctive talent for holistic value based sports education and help our valuable members understand sports ethics and the habit of fair play.

Equipment & Facilities

We provide state of the art facilities and equipment that enable us to provide the best service to our distinguished members thereby ensuring supreme satisfaction.

Certified  Coaches

Our team of experienced coaches and trainers consists of industry professionals, former athletes, and sports enthusiasts who bring their expertise and passion to every session. We are dedicated to creating a supportive and inspiring environment that encourages athletes to push beyond their limits, develop strong character, and cultivate lifelong values such as discipline, teamwork, and resilience.

Wide   Sports Option
