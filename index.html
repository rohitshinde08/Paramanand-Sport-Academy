ExpertCoaches

Manav Phule Gymnastics coach A certified NIS and BPED coach, Dso national and Junior association national medalist,4 times All india university national participation as a player.Manav has over 6+ years of experience in Men’s artistic gymnastics 10+ student national medalist and 15+ state medalist and also 2 times Maharashtra team coach manager.
Pranav Sawai Badminton Coach Pranav Uday Sawai is an experienced badminton coach with over 6 years of coaching expertise. He holds the prestigious certification from Padukone Sports Management (PSM), which highlights his commitment to excellence in coaching. With a deep understanding of the game and a passion for developing talent, Coach Pranav guides aspiring badminton players to reach their full potential.
Raj Kale Gymnastics Coach A senior national medalist and he has also represented Maharashtra state at various national competitions and bagged medals ,raj has over 10+ years of experience in artistic gymnastics and has taught gymnastics at Ashoka Universal School Nashik.
Santosh Bhalerao Mallakhamba Coach Mallakhamba, a traditional Indian sport, requires skilled coaches who have extensive experience in training athletes in this discipline. One such accomplished coach is Santosh Bhalerao, who boasts more than 20 years of experience in Mallakhamba coaching.
Rahul Fadol Fencing coach A certified National Level Coach from Netaji Subhash National Institute of Sports,Rahul has over six years of experience in Fencing.He has also represented Maharashtra at various national competitions and bagged medals and has taught fencing in various institutions in nashik.
Sunil Deshmukh Football Coach sunil is an accomplished football coach with a wealth of experience and expertise in the field. They hold an AIFF licensed coach certification, demonstrating their commitment to professional coaching standards. Prior to transitioning into coaching, Sunil had a successful career as a state-level player, and has 4 years of experience in football coaching
Rahul Jadhav Basketball Coach Rahul Jadhav, an accomplished basketball coach, led the Nashik District Basketball U-16 boys' team to a commendable third-place finish in the 2012 inter-district state level championship. As a former player, he secured second place in the 2015-16 west zone inter-university championship while representing Savitribai Phule Pune University. This success earned him a spot in the All India inter-university championship held at Mumbai University.
Pushkar Jadhav Chess FA Pushkar Jadhav is a highly experienced chess coach at Parmanand Sports Academy. With the title of FIDE Arbiter and a FIDE Rating of 1232, Coach Pushkar brings extensive knowledge and expertise to his students.
