Chess ♟

Welcome to our Chess Section at Parmanand Sports Academy !


Chess is a timeless game that challenges the mind, enhances critical thinking, and cultivates strategic decision-making skills. At Parmanand Sports Academy , we are delighted to provide a dedicated Chess Section where players of all ages and skill levels can engage in the fascinating world of chess.


Our Chess Section is led by a team of experienced and passionate chess coaches who are committed to nurturing chess enthusiasts and helping them reach their full potential. Whether you're a beginner learning the fundamentals or an advanced player seeking to refine your skills, our academy offers a supportive and intellectually stimulating environment for chess players to thrive.


We provide excellent chess facilities designed to enhance the learning and playing experience. Our chess rooms are equipped with comfortable seating, chess sets, clocks, and digital boards to facilitate interactive learning and competitive play. We create an ambiance that inspires concentration and strategic thinking, fostering a love for the game among our members.


Contact us today to learn more about our chess programs, schedule a trial session, or to inquire about any additional information. Let's sharpen our minds, make strategic moves, and experience the intellectual thrill of chess at Parmanand Sports Academy !
